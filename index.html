31-band parametric EQ

Shape the sound. Hear it as you drag.

Drag a point and the change is live in the music. Pick your headphones and a target, and AutoEQ builds the correction for you. Presets import and export in the Squiglink format.

  • AutoEQ for 6,800+ headphones and IEMs
  • Undo, redo and a full history
  • Preamp warns you before anything can clip

Lyrics that find themselves

Sing along, line by line.

Synced lyrics follow the music and a click jumps to any line. When a track has none, TeleCloud looks them up for you, and you choose what gets saved.

  • Searches LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music and more, in the order you set
  • Edit by hand, save into the file's tag or a .lrc next to it
  • Reads existing .lrc files, and backs lyrics up with your library

Your listening, measured

A dashboard for the music you actually play.

Every play is counted, by day, week, month or year: your top tracks, artists and albums, when you listen, and how much of it is hi-res.

  • Play counts on every track, and a "Most played" sort
  • Last.fm scrobbling, with "now playing" on your profile
  • Artist biographies and similar artists on every artist page

And there's more

The details that make it yours.

Tag editor

Fix titles, artists, albums and disc numbers for one track or a whole album at once. Backed-up files follow.

Select many, act once

Ctrl/Shift+click and Ctrl+A in every list: queue, favourite, back up, tag or remove together.

Spectrum check

See the whole file's frequencies and spot upsampled or lossy-sourced "hi-res", with a clear explanation of each verdict.

Albums, discs, links

Multi-disc albums split by disc. Any artist or album name opens its page, from anywhere in the app.

Cover-only mode

One key hides everything but the artwork, for when the music is the only thing on screen.

Updates itself

New versions download in the background and install on the next restart. Beta users get betas first.
