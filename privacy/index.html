Privacy

Your music stays yours.

TeleCloud is an app on your computer, not a service. There is no TeleCloud server, no account with us, and nothing is collected about you or how you use the app.

Last updated 20 November 2026

What stays on your computer

  • Your library, playlists, play counts, listening history, settings and logs.
  • Your Telegram sign-in and the key that encrypts your backup. Both are protected by Windows itself (the data protection tied to your Windows account), and never leave the computer except as you choose, for example when you write the recovery key down.
  • The log, when something goes wrong. It is only sent if you send it yourself, and passwords, keys and your Telegram session are never in it.

What goes to your own Telegram

If you turn on TeleCloud Sync, your music is uploaded to a private channel in your Telegram account, signed in with an API ID you create yourself. Files are encrypted on your computer before they are sent (this is on by default), so Telegram stores data it cannot read. TeleCloud's developer has no access to your account or your files. See TeleCloud and Telegram and Security.

What TeleCloud never does

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data: there is none to share.
  • No use of your music, messages or Telegram data to train AI.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which keeps the usual server logs, and its fonts come from Google Fonts.
