TeleCloud Beta turns your phone, tablet or another computer into a remote for the music playing on your PC. Nothing to install on it, and the sound never leaves your DAC.

Remote Flex Mode Lyrics Wake-on-LAN
TeleCloud Beta

Nothing to install

Scan the QR code in TeleCloud and the remote opens in the phone's browser. It works the same on a tablet or another computer.

The sound stays put

The remote only sends commands. Your PC still plays every file to your DAC, bit-perfect, with the signal path to prove it.

Only for your devices

A pairing key in the QR code, only on your own network. Set a device to view only, or sign every one out with a new key.

TeleCloud Remote

Scan once. It stays paired.

Settings › Remote shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone and the remote opens; next time it just connects, even after the address changes.

Every control, in your hand.

Play, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat and volume, with the cover and the quality of the file. Exclusive mode stays locked at 100%, as on the PC.

Your whole library, on the sofa.

Search, albums, artists, playlists, favourites and recently played, a page at a time even with 10,000 tracks. Play now, play next or add to the queue.

Lyrics that follow along.

The words light up as they are sung, from the lyrics in your files. Tap a line to jump there.

The output, the EQ, the signal path.

Switch to your DAC, turn the EQ on or off, and see in short what happens to the sound on the way there.

Made for Galaxy Z Fold

Open it like a book.

On the inner screen, two pages meet at the crease: now playing in the album's colours on the left, the queue, library and output on the right. Nothing sits on the fold.

Half fold it. Flex Mode.

The cover or the lyrics fill the top half, big controls the bottom half, right where your thumbs are. Open it flat and the book comes back.

Closed, the tabs come down.

On the tall cover screen the tabs sit at the bottom, under your thumb. Fold 5, 6, 7 and 8, the Fold 8 Ultra too.

Any screen, its own layout

A tablet gets two columns. A computer, three.

The same remote fits whatever opens it: tabs on a phone, now playing beside the queue on a tablet, output, queue and player side by side in a browser on another PC.

  • Follows the theme you use in TeleCloud
  • Tag, cover and artist picture changes show up straight away
  • Tracks only in TeleCloud stream as they do on the PC
  • Press and hold a long name to read it in full
TeleCloud Remote

Stays reachable

No sleep in the middle of an album.

Keep the PC awake while music plays, so the remote can always reach it. And if it does sleep, TeleCloud shows the steps and the MAC address to wake it from a phone with Wake-on-LAN.

  • Your PC's own name on every remote, or one you choose
  • View only for devices that should just watch
  • New key signs every device out at once
