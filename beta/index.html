TeleCloud Beta

Nothing to install

Scan the QR code in TeleCloud and the remote opens in the phone's browser. It works the same on a tablet or another computer.

The sound stays put

The remote only sends commands. Your PC still plays every file to your DAC, bit-perfect, with the signal path to prove it.

Only for your devices

A pairing key in the QR code, only on your own network. Set a device to view only, or sign every one out with a new key.

TeleCloud Remote

Scan once. It stays paired.

Settings › Remote shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone and the remote opens; next time it just connects, even after the address changes.

Every control, in your hand.

Play, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat and volume, with the cover and the quality of the file. Exclusive mode stays locked at 100%, as on the PC.

Your whole library, on the sofa.

Search, albums, artists, playlists, favourites and recently played, a page at a time even with 10,000 tracks. Play now, play next or add to the queue.

Lyrics that follow along.

The words light up as they are sung, from the lyrics in your files. Tap a line to jump there.

The output, the EQ, the signal path.

Switch to your DAC, turn the EQ on or off, and see in short what happens to the sound on the way there.

Made for Galaxy Z Fold

Open it like a book.

On the inner screen, two pages meet at the crease: now playing in the album's colours on the left, the queue, library and output on the right. Nothing sits on the fold.

Half fold it. Flex Mode.

The cover or the lyrics fill the top half, big controls the bottom half, right where your thumbs are. Open it flat and the book comes back.

Closed, the tabs come down.

On the tall cover screen the tabs sit at the bottom, under your thumb. Fold 5, 6, 7 and 8, the Fold 8 Ultra too.

Settings › Sound

Warm or bright, with one slider.

Warmth tilts the whole sound around 1 kHz. Bass, Treble and Loudness, stereo width from mono to wider than the record, mono, swap and polarity. Night mode, Air and Tube warmth when you want a little colour.

Watch the music move.

The parametric EQ now has a live spectrum under the curve: the music as it plays, after every filter, so you see what a band really does.

A red light when it clips.

Boost too far and the sound goes over 0 dBFS. The light in the signal path blinks red and says what to do. Prevent clipping lowers the level by your biggest boost, so it never gets there.

Hear the difference, fairly.

A/B plays the original at the same loudness, because louder always sounds better. Load a room or headphone correction from REW, Dirac or a text curve, and compare.

Sing along

Word by word, even when the file only has lines.

Words light up as they are sung: in Now Playing, the floating lyrics and the remote. NetEase, QQ Music and Kugou give their word-by-word lyrics, and for the rest Cadence works the timing out on your computer, from the song itself. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Cadence is optional: download its models only if you want them
  • Fix any word by dragging it, or tap Space as it is sung
  • Floating lyrics stay on top of every app, clicks go through when locked
  • Enhanced LRC and TTML, as other players use them
TeleCloud Beta

Your own backup

No server. No subscription. Encrypted before it leaves.

Back up your library to your own Telegram, or now to a folder: a second drive, a share on your NAS, or the folder your OneDrive or Google Drive app keeps in step. Encrypted on your PC with your recovery key, streamed back to any computer with seeking.

  • No Telegram account needed for a folder; move tracks between the two either way
  • Restore on a new computer: the library comes back with tags, lyrics and covers
  • Export your history, playlists and favourites to plain files; nothing keeps you here
  • Import adds, never replaces, and missing tracks join their playlists when they arrive

Your library, put to use

Playlists that fill themselves. A year ago, today.

Write a few rules (artist, genre, quality, plays, last played, favourite, on this PC or in TeleCloud) and a smart playlist keeps itself up to date. Discover brings back your most played and what you were listening to on this day in earlier years.

  • Folders: your library as it sits on disk, play a whole folder
  • .m3u8 in and out; tracks not found are listed, never dropped quietly
  • Built from your own play history, on your computer

And the small things

VU meters you can watch.

Two analogue needles in Now Playing, in four faces, one in the colours of your theme. Set 0 VU where you like, and a peak light warns near full scale. They only watch: bit-perfect stays bit-perfect.

The next cover floods in.

A new album's cover floods in as a growing circle, and the backdrop behind Now Playing drifts in its colours like ink in water, then settles.

Media keys. A sleep timer.

Your keyboard's media keys, the Windows flyout and lock screen, the taskbar and the tray. Fall asleep to an album: minutes, the end of a track or the album, and the last seconds fade out.

Settings that find themselves.

Type a word in Settings, or pick Bit-perfect, DSD or Lyrics, and every matching setting shows from all the tabs. Pick one and Settings opens right there.

Any screen, its own layout.

The remote gives a phone tabs, a tablet two columns and a browser on another PC three. TeleCloud keeps the PC awake while it plays, and shows how to wake it with Wake-on-LAN.

Ready for your language.

Every text in TeleCloud, the tray and the remote can be translated, and changes in place without a restart. English is in the app today; translators are welcome on GitHub.
