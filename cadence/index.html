05 / THE WORD BY WORD EDITOR

Time a chorus once. Paste it on every repeat.

Cadence lives in the lyrics window, beside the line-synced editor. Run it on the whole song or only the lines without word times, watch each stage, stop it any time.

  • Drag a word's edge, or nudge it with the arrow keys
  • Tap Space on each word as it is sung, while the song plays
  • Copy word times with Ctrl+C and paste them onto a repeated line
  • Undo and redo everything; fix a word's spelling with Enter
  • Works on tracks kept only in TeleCloud too

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR PC

The song is read and timed on your computer. No account, no upload, no server of ours, because there is none.

LIGHT WHEN IDLE

It runs in its own process for one song and closes when it is done, so its memory goes with it. On the graphics card through DirectML, or on the processor.

Cadence is built on open research: Demucs for separating the voice (MIT) and wav2vec 2.0 for hearing the words (Apache-2.0), both first published by Meta's AI research, run with ONNX Runtime. What the packs contain and how they are made is on GitHub.
