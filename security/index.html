Security

Encrypted before it leaves your computer.

With encryption on, which is the default, your backup is encrypted on your computer with a key only you have. Telegram stores it, but cannot read it. The code that does this is open, in electron/telecloud/crypto.js.

Last updated 20 November 2026

Your key

  • One random 256-bit master key is made on your computer when you set up TeleCloud Sync. It is never sent anywhere.
  • You see it as a recovery key of 52 characters in groups of four, written without look-alike characters (no 0/O or 1/I/L), so it survives being copied by hand. Another computer needs it to open your backup.
  • On your computer, the key, your Telegram sign-in and your API ID are protected by Windows' data protection (DPAPI), tied to your Windows account.
  • Separate keys for files and for track info are derived from the master key (HKDF-SHA256), so neither is used for two jobs.

What is encrypted, and how

WhatHow
The music filesAES-256-CTR with a random IV per file. The encrypted file is the same size as the original, and any part of it can be decrypted on its own, so a track streams and seeks without downloading it all.
Track info (title, artist, album, checksum)AES-256-GCM with a random nonce, so a wrong key or a changed message is caught.
The library index and album coversEncrypted with the same key, before upload.
File namesReplaced by a short random-looking name; the real name is inside the encrypted track info.

Every file carries a short fingerprint of the key it was encrypted with, so a wrong recovery key is noticed before anything is decrypted. A file downloaded in full is checked against the SHA-256 checksum of the original, so the copy you play is exactly the file you backed up.

What Telegram can see

  • That your account has a channel with files in it, how many, how big, and when they were uploaded.
  • That the messages come from TeleCloud (their captions start with a short marker).
  • Not the music, the titles, the artists, the album covers or your library.

If you turn encryption off, files are uploaded as they are, with their names and track info readable in the channel.

What encryption does not cover

  • Your computer. Anyone who controls your Windows account, or malware running in it, can use TeleCloud as you can.
  • Your Telegram account. Someone who takes over your account cannot read the files, but could delete them. Turn on Telegram's two-step verification.
  • Losing the key. There is no way around a lost recovery key, by design. Keep it somewhere outside this computer.
  • Sizes and times. These are visible to Telegram, as above.

The Remote

The Remote works only inside your home network. A device pairs with a one-time key from the QR code or link shown in TeleCloud, then keeps its own token in a cookie the page's scripts cannot read; TeleCloud stores only a hash of each token. Devices can be made watch-only, or removed, and a new key signs every device out.

Reporting a problem

If you find a security problem, please report it privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting rather than in a public issue. You will get an answer as soon as the developer can, and credit in the release notes if you would like it.
